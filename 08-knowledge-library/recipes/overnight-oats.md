# Overnight oats

**Serves:** 1
**Prep time:** 5 minutes
**Chill time:** 8 hours (overnight in the fridge)
**Total time:** 8 hours 5 minutes

## Ingredients

- 50 g rolled oats
- 120 ml milk
- 2 tablespoons yogurt
- 1 teaspoon honey
- A handful of berries

## Steps

1. Put the oats, milk, yogurt and honey in a jar.
2. Stir well and close the lid.
3. Leave the jar in the fridge overnight.
4. In the morning, stir again and top with the berries.

## Notes

Only 5 minutes of work, but you must start the night before.
