# Pancakes

**Serves:** 4
**Prep time:** 10 minutes
**Cook time:** 15 minutes
**Total time:** 25 minutes

## Ingredients

- 200 g plain flour
- 2 teaspoons baking powder
- 1 tablespoon sugar
- A pinch of salt
- 300 ml milk
- 1 egg
- 2 tablespoons melted butter, plus a little for the pan

## Steps

1. Mix the flour, baking powder, sugar and salt in a bowl.
2. Whisk the milk, egg and melted butter in a jug.
3. Pour the wet mix into the dry mix. Stir until just combined. A few lumps are fine.
4. Heat a pan over medium heat and add a little butter.
5. Pour in about 3 tablespoons of batter per pancake.
6. Flip when bubbles appear on top, after about 2 minutes. Cook 1 minute more.

## Notes

Keep cooked pancakes warm in a low oven while you make the rest.
