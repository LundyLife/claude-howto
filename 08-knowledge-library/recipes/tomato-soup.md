# Tomato soup

**Serves:** 4
**Prep time:** 10 minutes
**Cook time:** 30 minutes
**Total time:** 40 minutes

## Ingredients

- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1 onion, chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, chopped
- 800 g tinned chopped tomatoes
- 500 ml vegetable stock
- 1 teaspoon sugar
- Salt and pepper
- A handful of fresh basil

## Steps

1. Warm the oil in a large pot over medium heat.
2. Cook the onion for 8 minutes, until soft. Add the garlic for 1 minute.
3. Add the tomatoes, stock and sugar. Bring to a simmer.
4. Simmer for 20 minutes.
5. Blend until smooth. Season with salt and pepper.
6. Tear in the basil and serve.

## Notes

This soup freezes well for up to three months.
