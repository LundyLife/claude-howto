# Vegetable stir-fry

**Serves:** 2
**Prep time:** 10 minutes
**Cook time:** 5 minutes
**Total time:** 15 minutes

## Ingredients

- 1 tablespoon cooking oil
- 1 red pepper, sliced
- 1 carrot, cut into thin sticks
- 150 g broccoli, in small pieces
- 2 spring onions, sliced
- 2 tablespoons soy sauce
- 1 teaspoon honey
- Cooked rice or noodles, to serve

## Steps

1. Mix the soy sauce and honey in a cup.
2. Heat the oil in a wok or large pan over high heat.
3. Add the carrot and broccoli. Stir for 2 minutes.
4. Add the pepper and spring onions. Stir for 2 minutes more.
5. Pour in the sauce and toss for 1 minute.
6. Serve over rice or noodles.

## Notes

Cut everything before you start. The cooking goes fast.
