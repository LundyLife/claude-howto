# Roast chicken

**Serves:** 4
**Prep time:** 15 minutes
**Cook time:** 1 hour 20 minutes
**Rest time:** 10 minutes
**Total time:** 1 hour 45 minutes

## Ingredients

- 1 whole chicken, about 1.5 kg
- 1 lemon, cut in half
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 4 cloves garlic
- A few sprigs of thyme
- Salt and pepper

## Steps

1. Heat the oven to 200 °C (400 °F).
2. Put the lemon halves, garlic and thyme inside the chicken.
3. Rub the skin with the oil. Season well with salt and pepper.
4. Roast for 1 hour 20 minutes, until the juices run clear.
5. Rest for 10 minutes under foil before you carve.

## Notes

Save the bones to make stock.
